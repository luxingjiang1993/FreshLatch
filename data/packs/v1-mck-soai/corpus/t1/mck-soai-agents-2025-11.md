## p1
本文件为 McKinsey State of AI（2025-11 agents 波）公开洞察**摘录**，用于顾问报告样例包的 T1 语料。完整报告 PDF 不进入本仓库开源语料。

## p2
公开洞察写明：88 percent report regular AI use in at least one business function，compared with 78 percent a year ago。企业层面多数仍处 experimenting or piloting；约三分之一称已开始 scale AI programs。相对 T0「约 78%」口径，采用率数字已更新。

## p3
关于 AI agents：Twenty-three percent of respondents report their organizations are scaling an agentic AI system somewhere in their enterprises，and an additional 39 percent say they have begun experimenting with AI agents。Key findings 亦写明 Sixty-two percent of survey respondents say their organizations are at least experimenting with AI agents。任一给定业务职能内，scaling AI agents 的受访者比例不超过 10 percent。

## p4
价值捕获：Respondents report use-case-level cost and revenue benefits，and 64 percent say that AI is enabling their innovation。However, just 39 percent report EBIT impact at the enterprise level。Redesigning workflows is a key success factor：高绩效者更常从根本上重新设计工作流，且更常以增长/创新为目标而非仅效率。

## p5
本波明确把 agents、创新与企业级价值捕获并列为调查主线；相对 T0 rewiring 波，agents 采用与「仍多在试点」成为可点回的 T1 新事实，可用于对照此前未写入 agents 规模化率的签发主张。
